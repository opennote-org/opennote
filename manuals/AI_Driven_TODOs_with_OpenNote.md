# AI Driven TODOs with OpenNote

Use AI to review and update your OpenNote TODOs. Keep writing rules alongside your tasks so AI can follow them consistently.

## How can you structure your markdown

This is a template that I use to manage my todos with AI. You may copy and paste it and make your own modifications. 

```
# TODOs

## How To View and Write Here

Work Folder: ~/Works

Write each todo in one or two sentences. Split larger tasks into
multiple todos. Preserve important context and checkbox status.

## Items

- [ ] Project name
  - [ ] Implement a specific feature.
  - [ ] Verify the feature works.
  - [ ] Resolve a blocker. Waiting for access from the owning team.
```

## How to use

Review without editing:

> Read my OpenNote TODOs and summarize open tasks and blockers. Don’t change anything.

Add a task:

> Under ChatBMW scout, add a todo to verify service health after deployment. Follow the note’s writing instructions.

Shorten long items:

> Find overly long todos and rewrite them concisely. Split distinct actions into separate todos without losing important details.

Update progress:

> Mark BATW setup complete and leave the remaining BATW tasks unchecked.

Keep edits scoped:

> Update only the Deploydesk todo to say BATW validation is blocked by missing dependencies. Leave everything else unchanged and confirm the saved edit.
